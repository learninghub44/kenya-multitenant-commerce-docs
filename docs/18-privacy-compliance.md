# Privacy and Compliance Checklist

Engineering checklist, not legal advice. Confirm current Kenyan and applicable international requirements with qualified counsel.

Inventory customer/staff identifiers, phone/email, delivery addresses, order history, support messages, audit logs, payment references, and uploads. Document purpose, access roles, retention, and deletion/export behavior.

Controls: collect only necessary information; publish accurate privacy notices; define data-subject request handling; restrict and audit access/exports; protect data in transit and at rest; define retention for accounts, orders, logs, and backups; review processors and cross-border processing; establish breach response; follow applicable marketing consent/opt-out rules; review consumer protection, electronic transactions, tax, and recordkeeping requirements.

Never store M-PESA PINs or card data. Clarify merchant-of-record responsibilities, receipts, refunds, settlement reports, and merchant recordkeeping.
