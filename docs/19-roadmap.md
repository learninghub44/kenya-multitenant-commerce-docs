# Roadmap

## Phase 0: foundation
Confirm brand/domain, hosting wildcard support, provider onboarding, and legal assumptions. Establish CI, environments, migrations, threat model, and acceptance tests. Build auth, tenant creation, membership, roles, and RLS.

## Phase 1: sell online
Store profile/subdomain, product/category/variant management, image uploads, publication, storefront, cart/checkout, order snapshots, stock reservations, one provider in sandbox with verified callbacks and reconciliation, fulfilment, audit, notifications, basic reports.

## Phase 2: retail ERP
Stocktakes, low-stock alerts, suppliers, purchase orders, partial goods receipts, locations/transfers if needed, expenses, CSV import/export, and bulk product updates.

## Phase 3: growth
Second provider after contract tests; custom domains, permission customization, advanced reports, coupons/invoices, CRM/loyalty, more notification channels, integrations, and country-specific currency/tax configurations after discovery.

Prioritize correctness and reliability over feature count. Avoid payroll, manufacturing, complex accounting, and microservices until core storefront, stock, and payments are dependable.
