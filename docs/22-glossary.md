# Glossary

- Tenant: a business workspace whose data and settings are isolated.
- Storefront: public website used to browse and buy products.
- Merchant console: private workspace for store operations.
- SKU: stock-keeping identifier for a product variant.
- Variant: sellable option such as size or color.
- On hand: physical stock recorded at a location.
- Reserved stock: stock committed to open checkout/orders.
- Available stock: quantity sellable under configured policy.
- Stock movement: append-only record of quantity change and reason.
- Stocktake: physical count compared with recorded quantity.
- Goods received note: record of inventory physically received.
- Idempotency: retrying a logical request does not repeat its effect.
- Webhook/callback: server-to-server provider event.
- Reconciliation: compare platform and provider records to find discrepancies.
- RLS: database row security policies.
- Merchant settlement: provider payout to merchant's configured account, subject to provider terms.
- MVP: smallest dependable release that solves the core problem.
